# Project Charter

## Enterprise Agentic AI Service Desk

Version: v0.1.0

---

# Executive Summary

Enterprise Agentic AI Service Desk is an intelligent ITSM platform that leverages Generative AI, Multi-Agent Systems, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to automate enterprise service desk operations.

The platform is designed to reduce operational effort, improve SLA compliance, and accelerate incident resolution.

---

# Business Problem

Organizations receive thousands of IT support requests every day.

Challenges include:

- Manual ticket routing
- Slow incident resolution
- Poor knowledge discovery
- High operational costs
- Repetitive support activities

---

# Project Goal

Develop an Enterprise AI platform capable of:

- AI Copilot
- Multi-Agent Collaboration
- Knowledge Search
- Incident Resolution
- Service Request Automation
- Analytics Dashboard
- Azure Deployment
